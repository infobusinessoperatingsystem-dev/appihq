# appigh
Individuals, with skills, almost always find themselves working under the leadership of someone, an "employer", who's harsh and cruel to them almost all the time. What can you do when someone with more power portray such behaviour towards you in an environment with no systems to deal with such situation? See, there's a reason for a system to exist when two or more people decide to do something together. One of them must become the leader.

The alternative is to apply for another job elsewhere. If you you're lucky, you'll find one with better pay, great working conditions and better benefits.

For some reason you just lost your job. You're unemployed.
{
    I'd first assess my financial situation, calculate how long I can survive without income, and cut unnecessary expenses.

    Then I'd identify skills I can monetize, find people with problems I can solve, and focus on getting my first paying customers rather than immediately looking for another job.

    Once I have consistent income, I'd build a repeatable business around what works.

    My goal wouldn't just be to replace my lost salary. It would be to build multiple sources of income so my livelihood doesn't depend on a single employer.
}

A better option now is to find another job but you know it's not going to be any different.
